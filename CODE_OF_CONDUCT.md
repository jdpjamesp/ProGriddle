# Code of conduct

ProGriddle is a small, friendly project about making grid based games in OpenEdge ABL. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity. This page says how we treat each other.

## Our standard

In issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain rather than scold.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers. A question about ABL, OpenEdge or the framework is always fine.
- Own your mistakes, and learn from them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, message the maintainer, [@jdpjamesp](https://github.com/jdpjamesp), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
